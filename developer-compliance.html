Developer Compliance (Pinterest API)

Last reviewed: March 23, 2026

This page maps Pinterest Developer/API Terms to Moodsearch implementation decisions.

Allowed use and consent

  • Use data only for authorized end user flows.
  • No hidden actions on behalf of users.
  • No automated engagement or bulk actions.
Implemented in app UX and compliance notice card.

Data storage and sharing

  • No permanent storage of API-fetched Pinterest data (except campaign analytics where allowed).
  • No sale or broker sharing of Pinterest data.
  • No cross-service identity/data merging.
Declared in privacy policy and internal compliance checklist.

Security and incident response

  • Protect API credentials with industry-standard controls.
  • Rapid incident handling and notification obligations.
Operational runbook and automated breach alert escalation should be finalized before production launch.

Technical restrictions

  • No scraping or bypassing Pinterest controls.
  • No reverse engineering of Pinterest materials.
  • Follow API updates and version requirements.
Added to compliance checklist and engineering guidance.

Children and sensitive use

  • No service intended for children under 13.
  • No deriving non-public personal information (e.g., hidden location inference).
