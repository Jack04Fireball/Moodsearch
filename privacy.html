Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 23, 2026

Moodsearch is an independent project by Jeremia Flammer. This policy explains how Moodsearch handles Pinterest-related data in line with Pinterest Developer/API Terms and applicable privacy laws.

1. Data we access

  • OAuth-based account authorization data needed to connect Pinterest.
  • User-selected boards and Pins used for style matching.
  • Technical logs required for security and service reliability.

2. Purpose limitation

Pinterest data is used only to provide features for the same authorized user who connected Pinterest. We do not use Pinterest data for unrelated profiling or external ad targeting.

3. Storage and retention

Except for campaign analytics tied to your own account where applicable, we do not permanently store API-fetched Pinterest data. Data is processed temporarily to generate results and is then discarded according to operational retention limits.

4. Sharing restrictions

  • No sale of Pinterest data.
  • No broker-style data sharing.
  • No combining Pinterest data with personal data from other services for cross-service profiling.

5. Consent and user control

Pinterest access is based on explicit authorization. Users can revoke access through Pinterest account settings. Where required by law, device-level consent (cookies/local storage or similar technologies) is requested before use.

6. Security

We use industry-standard security controls to protect API credentials and data in transit. Access controls are limited to authorized personnel and systems.

7. Data breach response

In case of a confirmed breach impacting Pinterest data, we will contain, investigate, mitigate, and notify Pinterest without undue delay, including accelerated timelines where required by law.

8. Prohibited uses

  • No scraping or unauthorized data extraction.
  • No hidden automation of Pinterest actions on behalf of users.
  • No action without user-specific intent and consent.
  • No use intended for children under 13.
Notice: Moodsearch is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pinterest.
