Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 23, 2026

Moodsearch is an independent project by Jeremia Flammer. This Privacy Policy explains how Moodsearch handles information when users connect their Pinterest account and use Pinterest content within the app.

1. What data we access

If a user chooses to connect Pinterest, Moodsearch may access limited Pinterest account data made available through the official Pinterest API, such as:

  • basic account information required for authentication,
  • boards selected by the user,
  • Pins selected by the user for inspiration and search purposes.

2. How we use Pinterest data

Pinterest data is used only to provide the core functionality of Moodsearch: allowing users to use their own Pinterest boards and selected Pins as visual inspiration for search and creative discovery inside the app.

Pinterest data is used only in the context of the authorized user.

3. Data storage

We do not store Pinterest data permanently and only process it temporarily to provide the service.

We do not sell Pinterest data, and we do not use Pinterest data for third-party advertising.

4. Data sharing

Moodsearch does not sell, rent, or share Pinterest data with third parties except where required by law or where necessary to operate the service in a lawful and secure manner.

5. User control

Users choose whether to connect their Pinterest account. Access to Pinterest data is based on user authorization and can be revoked by the user through Pinterest or the app, where applicable.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect data and to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

7. Third-party services

Moodsearch may rely on third-party infrastructure or hosting services to operate the app or website. Those providers may process technical data as needed to deliver their services.
