Moodsearch

Moodsearch is an independent prototype that uses Pinterest boards as style inspiration to suggest products.

How it works

Users connect Pinterest, select moodboards, and receive ranked product suggestions. Designer/brand preferences are treated as soft focus signals, not hard exclusion rules.

Data and compliance

Moodsearch applies Pinterest Developer/API restrictions for consent, data use, storage limits, security, and non-scraping behavior.

Read the full Privacy Policy and the Developer Compliance Page.
